Every dropped folder becomes a batch: its shots plus the parameters you enter for it. The shot current is the mean of the current channel (editable per shot). All batches form one pool that is saved in this browser; Export pool moves it to another computer.

Metrics (per edge, between the levels before and after it)

LevelsMedian before the edge and over the second half of the time until the next edge; swing = difference
Slew 10–90 / 20–80 %0.8 (0.6) × swing / time between the level crossings; 10–90 % time = rise/fall time
Peak dv/dtLargest least-squares slope over the dv/dt window between the 10 % and 90 % crossings
OvershootFurthest excursion beyond the final level within the ringing window (3-point smoothed)
Ringing pk-pkFirst oscillation after the 50 % crossing: first peak minus the following valley (smoothed)
Ringing frequencyFrom the spacing of the ringing peaks and valleys after the first peak (half a period each, up to 4 periods); empty when there is less than one full period

! marks shots or edges whose values may be unreliable (no edge, clipped, level not settled); hover it for the reason.
